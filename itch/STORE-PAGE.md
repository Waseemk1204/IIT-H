## Title

## Short description (tagline)

A comic jugaad stealth game: one power cut, one strict warden, one plate of Maggi.

## Description

It's 1:30 AM, the night before the Endsem. The hostel gate has been chained since 11, your
stomach is growling, and the whole block just went dark: load-shedding. Bhaiya's Maggi
Point outside the gate is open till 3. Warden Saab is awake, and he has a torch.

Sneak out of your room, down the wing, past the warden's torch and through the gates using
nothing but what a hostel has lying around.

- **Light is everything.** Darkness hides you. His torch beam doesn't. Neither does yours.
- **Jugaad, not keys.** Search almirahs, beds and tables. Bobby pin + compass = lock pick.
  Hanger + steel scale = lambi kundi. Every lock has more than one way through.
- **Distract him.** Throw a steel glass, leave your phone with an alarm, switch on the
  common-room radio, or knock on a sleeping student's door.
- **The twist.** Halfway through, the power comes back. Tube lights everywhere, twenty
  students cramming in the corridors, and Warden Saab can see the whole corridor. Hide in
  plain sight in a study circle, or jugaad the power off again.
- **Comic all the way.** Toon-shaded 3D with ink outlines, sound effects that pop up in the
  world ("KHATAK!", "DHADAAM!", "SLURRRP"), and comic-page cutscenes.

Get caught and you're marched back to your room. Get caught too often and... Papa ko phone.

**Controls (keyboard + mouse):** WASD move · mouse look · Shift run (loud!) · C crouch ·
F phone torch · E use (hold for longer jobs) · 1–6 pick an item · G combine · Q drop ·
click / V throw · P pause · M mute.
**Phone:** landscape, joystick on the left, drag on the right to look, on-screen buttons.

Three difficulties (Aasaan, Theek-thaak, Warden Mode). A short tutorial runs the first time.
About 10–15 minutes.

Made solo for the IIT-H Game Jam (themes: Comic · Twist · Light) by Mohammad Waseem Khan.
Built from scratch in plain JavaScript and Three.js. Source:
